## The Story, Briefly

Enterprise team revisit a planet, which allows a visit per 8 years because of ionization. In their visit, they discover a second William Riker. Then we see some developments regarding second Riker.

**Name of the episode:**

Giving a chance to both Riker (in terms of career development) and Riker-Dianna relationship.

## Thoughts

Certainly a cult episode. It's hard to think a second copy of yourself. Even while giving command to your copy, and knowing that how he will behave is an interesting tradeoff between behaving yourself and emphasizing yourself.
